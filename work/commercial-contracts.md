# Commercial contract judgment

## BMS licensing: separate ownership from use

I drafted and negotiated a cross-border patent and technology license for a battery-management system. The negotiation had stalled over ownership of improvements. I separated ownership from the rights needed to use the technology, then aligned those rights with sublicensing, transfer obligations and staged payments.

**Outcome:** the parties resolved the impasse, and both signed.

## Cloud services: align the cap with the remedy

I identified a general liability cap that could undermine a separately stated confidentiality remedy. My redline proposed excluding that remedy from the cap and broadening the trigger from disclosure to breach of the confidentiality obligations.

**Outcome:** the signed agreement raised the liability cap. The proposed positions and the confirmed signed result are distinct.

## Equipment procurement: connect acceptance and payment

I built a contract-by-contract record of delivery, acceptance evidence and payment milestones. The technical acceptance requirements included a 95% operating rate. I connected the record with the applicable cure and termination provisions and drafted default and termination notices.

**Delivered:** the procurement tracker and the notices, with the clause, date, evidence and next action kept together.

## Transaction execution and data permission

I wrote a 17-step Taiwan corporate-division plan and a 12-stage cross-border acquisition plan. Both transactions closed. At Lawsnote, I worked on the Ministry of Justice permission for the AI product's legal-data source; the permission was obtained.
